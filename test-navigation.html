Data Extractor Navigation Test

Step 1: Can you navigate to the Data Extractor?

Open http://localhost:8080

Click on "Data Extractor" tool card

Step 2: Are you on the Upload Files step?

You should see "Step 1 of 5: Upload Files" at the top

You should see two upload zones: "Master" and "Data"

Step 3: Upload files to both zones

Upload any Excel or CSV file to both Master and Data zones

The "Next: Configure Matching" button should become enabled

Step 4: Click Next to Configure step

Click "Next: Configure Matching" button

You should now see "Step 2 of 5: Configure Matching"

Step 5: Look for Navigation Buttons on Configure step

Scroll to the bottom of the Configure step

You should see:

  • Previous: Upload Files (always enabled)
  • Next: Preview Results (disabled until you select columns)

Step 6: Select columns to enable Next button

Click on at least one column from Master Spreadsheet

Click on at least one column from Data Spreadsheet

The "Next: Preview Results" button should become enabled
